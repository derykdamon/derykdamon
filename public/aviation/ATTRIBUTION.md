# HRL Aviation regional imagery

These photographs show actual locations, not our facilities, fleet, service inventory, or partners. Dates are historical and do not establish current conditions. No endorsement is implied.

- **Northern Harlingen aerial (2022):** RobertKixmiller. [Original and metadata](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aerial_View_Of_The_Northern_Part_Of_Harlingen,_TX_08202022_1.jpg). [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Photographed August 20, 2022 from a flight departing Valley International Airport. Files `harlingen-aerial-900.webp`, `harlingen-aerial-1600.webp`, `harlingen-aerial-2200.webp` are resized and compressed. Display crops, saturation, and overlay adaptations remain CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **South Padre Island beach (2022):** Spheroidite. [Original and metadata](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:South_Padre_Island_beach_panorama.jpg). [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). June 11, 2022; metadata location 26.117936, -97.166047. `padre-1600.webp` is resized and compressed. Display crops, saturation, and overlay adaptations remain CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **Laguna Atascosa NWR inlet (2003):** U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service Headquarters. [Original and metadata](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Laguna_Atascosa_NWR_inlet_(5121525224).jpg). Federal public-domain work, also published under [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). August 16, 2003. `laguna-inlet-1600.webp` is compressed; display crops and overlays are indicated in the page credit.

Map land polygons are clipped from Natural Earth's `ne_10m_land.geojson`, obtained from the [maintainer's repository](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_land.geojson) on October 7, 2026. [Public-domain terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Bounds: -98.12, 25.72, -96.88, 26.75. The map is a stylized regional illustration; connecting arcs are not operational routes. No terrain elevation, property boundaries, or navigation accuracy is represented.
